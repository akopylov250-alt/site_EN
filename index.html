Principles of Interior

Every project is built on the laws discovered by Vitruvius: durability, utility, and beauty.

Ⅰ

Proportion

The golden ratio, harmony of volumes and light. No line is accidental — everything is subject to the antique canon.

Ⅱ

Material

Natural textures and honest surfaces that age beautifully and retain their nobility over the years.

Ⅲ

History

Every interior is a dialogue with the past: from Roman mosaics to minimalism refined to its essence.

Ⅳ

Utility

Beauty without convenience is an empty form. Every solution is subject to the logic of life and the owners' scenarios.
